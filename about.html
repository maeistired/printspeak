The Problem

Labels shouldn't leave anyone guessing

Have you or someone you know ever struggled to read a product label? Maybe your medicine bottles look almost identical, or the shelves of canned goods are so visually loud that one is hard to tell from the next.

Whether it's the fonts, the colours, or the amount of information, many people with visual impairments or reduced eyesight find traditional flat labels difficult to read, because products offer so little variety in their visual information. Standard labels make it nearly impossible for many individuals to manage daily life independently.

Who We Are

A student-led initiative

PrintSpeak is a GCM student-led initiative at Toronto Metropolitan University, focused on using NFC and talking labels to improve accessibility in product labelling and design.

We help organizations branch out into NFC and talking-label technology to improve accessibility and daily operations, removing barriers for people with visual impairments and showing that smart technology can be a tool for human independence.

What We Do

More than research

We don't just study the problem. Through real-world field studies, insider data, and extensive research, we show how interactive talking labels can transform the way people interact with physical products.

Educate

Research

Advocate

We help organizations see accessibility as standard practice, not an add-on.
